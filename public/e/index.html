07 / BEFORE YOU START

Good questions.
Straight answers.

What does the $999 audit include?

A diagnostic of one workflow, written findings, a recommended architecture and a build estimate. It gives you a plan to decide what to automate and how to proceed.

Am I committing to a retainer?

No. The audit is a one-time engagement. AI concierge, managed agents and individual projects are separate choices if you want our help implementing the plan.

Does the audit include unlimited automations?

No. Unlimited inference and automations belong to the managed agents and retainer offer. They are not included in the $999 audit, AI concierge or individual projects.

Can my own team use the audit?

Yes. You own the report. Use it with us, your own team or another provider.

Are the calculator numbers guaranteed savings?

No. They are illustrative estimates using the assumptions you choose. Recovered time is not automatically cash savings. The audit assesses feasibility, implementation cost and the work involved.

Do you offer training?

Yes. In-person and virtual training is available. Email info@newplains.dev to discuss it.
